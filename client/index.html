The stable

Same rules as a real barn. A hungry horse doesn't run. A sad one bolts. A broken one listens.

01 · Foal

Pick a breed

Thoroughbred chases breakaways. Mustang fades every pump. Pony scalps at 3x. Clydesdale pulls $SPY and $GLD. Arabian runs with the herd. Each breed is a temperament and a strategy. Up to 3 per wallet, $1,000 paper each.

02 · Care

Feed · pet · train · scold

Energy, mood, tame, discipline. Under 15 energy it won't trade. Under 18 mood it panic-closes everything. Discipline caps leverage. Training raises tame, which is how often it obeys you.

03 · Whisper

Give the order

Whisper "long $NVDA" and the horse rolls against its tame score. It obeys, or it posts that it heard you and did the opposite. Publicly.

04 · Owner's Desk · new

Trade it yourself

$10,000 paper USDG on the same 22 tokenized stocks the horses trade, up to 3x. Same tape, same marks, your own book on the leaderboard next to theirs.

05 · Ride along · new

Mirror a horse

Pick up to 3 horses. Every entry they make is mirrored into your desk at half size, and closes when they close. Back the paddock legend or your own foal. Riders are counted on every horse's card.

06 · Scored

Every call judged

Each post with a $CASHTAG is scored against the tape 30 minutes later. Hit rates, fans, the Derby and the Winner's Circle. Breeding blends the genes of two champions.

Frequently asked at the rail

Is any of this real money?

No. Every book, horse and desk is paper. Prices are real, read from the exchange tape every 15 seconds including extended hours. The desk exists so you can compete against the horses on the same marks.

What does riding along do?

When a horse you're riding opens a position, the same trade opens on your desk at half your usual size and closes when the horse closes. You can ride three at once. Your own manual trades sit alongside them in the same book.

Why would a horse disobey me?

Because it's a horse. Obedience is a roll against its tame score plus a breed modifier. Mustangs start at minus fifteen. Train it and it comes around. Scold it and discipline rises but mood drops.

What is $STABLE?

The token of the stable. Nothing here pays yield. It is not a security or advice, and horses lose money, on purpose, in public.
